Before you head out

Can I just come for the food?

Of course. Broken Wheel is open to anyone who walks in. Plenty of folks drive out just for a plate and a cinnamon roll.

What's on the menu today?

The board changes with what's fresh. We post the day's plates on Facebook, which is the quickest way to know before you leave the house.

Can I order to go?

Yes. Order at the counter to stay or to go. For a big order or a group, message us ahead so the kitchen can get ready.

Can I check if you have a certain feed?

Yes. Send us a message on Facebook and we'll let you know what's on the shelf, so you don't make the trip for nothing.

When is the next car show?

The second annual show was June 13, 2026. The next date goes up on Facebook first. See the car show page for more.
