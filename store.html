The General & Feed StoreEst. 2025

Feed, staples, and a reason to stay in town.

A general store and feed counter on South Kansas Avenue, built because the nearest grocery is 26 miles one way.

When the nearest grocery is 26 miles away, you build a store in town.

That's the whole story of Schwarz General & Feed. Out here a forgotten bag of feed or a missing can of something for supper means an hour in the truck, round trip.

So we keep the essentials close: feed for the animals, the everyday things for the kitchen, and a hot meal from Broken Wheel while you're at it.

On the shelves

  • Livestock feedFor the herd out backFeed
  • Poultry feedFor the coop, laying or growingFeed
  • Dog & cat foodFor the ones who run the placePets
  • Everyday staplesSo the 26-mile drive can waitPantry
  • Hot food from scratchFrom Broken Wheel in the backKitchen

Shelves turn with the seasons and with what the farms around us need. Looking for something specific? Message us before you head out and we'll tell you if it's in.
