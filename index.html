The drive in. Every road out here is a long one.

When the grocery is 26 miles away, you build one in town.

Schwarz General & Feed Store opened on South Kansas Avenue in 2025 with a simple idea: the folks in and around Lane shouldn't have to burn a tank of gas for feed, staples, or a hot meal.

So there's a feed counter for the farms, shelves stocked with the everyday things, and a kitchen in the back called Broken Wheel, where nothing comes out of a box.
