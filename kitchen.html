Broken Wheel Scratch Kitchen

The kitchen inside the store. Everything is breaded, mixed, rolled and cooked right here. No heat-and-serve. Just real food.

Made from scratch. Every single day.

Broken Wheel started for the same reason the store did: the closest grocery is 26 miles one way, and nobody out here should have to settle for a microwave lunch.

So the dough gets kneaded by hand, the breading goes on one piece at a time, and the gravy starts in the skillet. It takes longer. It tastes like it.

The board changes with the season and with what's fresh. We post the day's plates on Facebook, so check before you make the drive.

Off the pass

A few of the plates folks come back for. Ask what's cooking when you walk in.

Sandwiches

The hand-breaded sandwich

Pounded thin, breaded by hand and fried until the crust cracks. It hangs off the bun on purpose. Lettuce, tomato and red onion keep it honest.

Plates

Country-fried with cream gravy

Crisp and golden, buried in peppered cream gravy, with real mashed potatoes and green beans on the side. The plate that started a lot of return trips.

Plates

Blackened fish over grits

Spice-crusted and seared hard, laid over buttery yellow grits with a charred lemon to squeeze over the top.

Mornings

The farm breakfast

Eggs how you like them, buttered toast and sausage links. Simple, hot, and ready before the chores are.

From the oven

Cinnamon rolls

Rolled from the same hand-worked dough and iced thick. Grab one for the road, or a box for the house. They go early.
